MacroTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

MacroTrack ("we", "our", "the app") is a nutrition and macro-tracking application. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how to contact us with questions.

Information we collect

To provide the app's core features, we collect and store:

  • Account information: the email address you sign up with.
  • Food logging data: foods you log, custom foods you create, recipes, quantities, dates, and daily log status (complete/partial/fasted).
  • Weight data: weight entries you log and the dates they were recorded.
  • Coaching data: derived expenditure estimates, macro targets, and weekly check-in responses, all calculated from the food and weight data above.
  • Favorites: foods, custom foods, or recipes you mark as favorites, to make them faster to find again.

Camera use

MacroTrack uses your device's camera for two optional features: scanning a product barcode, and scanning a nutrition information panel to pre-fill a custom food's details.

  • Barcode scanning happens entirely on your device. The camera feed is analyzed locally to read the barcode number; no image is stored or sent anywhere. Only the barcode number itself is used to look up a matching food.
  • Nutrition label scanning also happens entirely on your device, using on-device text recognition. The photo is processed in memory to extract text and is discarded immediately afterward - it is never saved to your device's storage or uploaded to us or anyone else. Only the nutrition values recognized from the photo (which you can review and edit before saving) are added to the custom food form.

Camera access is optional. Manual search and manual entry are always available without it.

How your information is used

Your data is used solely to provide MacroTrack's features to you: showing your logged history, calculating trends and expenditure estimates, and generating your macro targets and weekly check-ins. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

Where your information is stored

App data is stored using Supabase, a backend infrastructure provider that hosts our database on our behalf. Supabase processes data only as directed by MacroTrack and does not independently use it for its own purposes. Data is protected by row-level security policies that restrict each user's data to that user's own account.

Third-party sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your data is only accessible to our backend infrastructure provider (Supabase) as necessary to operate the app.

Your choices

You can delete individual log entries, custom foods, and recipes directly in the app. To request deletion of your entire account and all associated data, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

MacroTrack is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
